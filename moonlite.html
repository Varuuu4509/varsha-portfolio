STRUCTURAL PACKAGING

MOONLITE

EXHIBITION PACKAGING · STRUCTURAL DESIGN · VISUAL IDENTITY

Moonlite is a candle packaging design created for Trigon Digipack Pvt. Ltd. for exhibition display. Inspired by zodiac signs and celestial imagery, the design combines a rich purple palette with illustrated elements and a functional structure to create a distinctive and premium presentation.

THE VISUAL LANGUAGE

CELESTIAL STORYTELLING

ZODIAC ILLUSTRATION · COLOUR PALETTE · PANEL GRAPHICS

Zodiac symbols and celestial imagery shape the visual language of Moonlite. A deep purple palette, golden accents and illustrated details create a mystical character while connecting the packaging to its astrology-inspired theme.

PACKAGING GRAPHICS

DESIGN ACROSS PANELS

ZODIAC GRAPHICS · FRAGRANCE DETAILS · TYPOGRAPHY

Celestial graphics, fragrance details and typography extend across each panel, giving Moonlite a consistent and distinctive visual character.
