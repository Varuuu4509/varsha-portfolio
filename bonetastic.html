CONCEPTUAL PACKAGING

BONETASTIC

PET PACKAGING · CUSTOM STRUCTURE · VISUAL DESIGN

Bonetastic is a conceptual packaging design for dog and cat toys, built around a playful bone-shaped structure. The custom form, warm natural palette and lively pet imagery bring the product category into the packaging itself, creating a distinctive and approachable identity.

STRUCTURAL DEVELOPMENT

SHAPED AROUND THE IDEA

MARKET EXPLORATION · CUSTOM KEYLINE · BONE-SHAPED STRUCTURE

Initial market exploration highlighted an opportunity to move beyond conventional pet-toy packaging formats. This led to the development of a distinctive bone-shaped structure, turning the product category itself into the form of the pack. A custom keyline was then created to translate the concept into functional packaging.

FROM FLAT TO FORM

THE STRUCTURE IN USE

3D PACKAGING · FOLDING SYSTEM · FINAL FORM

The final 3D views demonstrate how the custom keyline transforms into a functional pack. The bone-shaped panels become part of the assembled structure while the graphics and product information remain integrated across different sides of the packaging.
