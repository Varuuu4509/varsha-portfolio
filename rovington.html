CONCEPT-LED BRAND IDENTITY

ROVINGTON

PACKAGING · BRAND IDENTITY · ILLUSTRATION · DIGITAL

A concept-led dog food brand designed for outdoor journeys, using a continuous leash-as-line illustration system across packaging and digital touchpoints.

THE VISUAL SYSTEM

FROM LINE TO IDENTITY

EXPLORATION

Different dog forms, movements and human–dog interactions were explored to develop the continuous line into a flexible visual system.

THE CONCEPT

TAKING A LINE FOR A WALK

LEASH - AS - LINE

Inspired by Paul Klee’s idea of “taking a line for a walk”, the dog’s leash becomes a continuous line connecting the identity across illustration, packaging and digital touchpoints.

THE IDENTITY

THE ROVINGTON MARK

LOGO DESIGN

The Rovington identity combines a playful wordmark with a distinctive dog-inspired brand mark. Tail-inspired details within the lettering subtly connect the identity to the pet category, creating a friendly and recognisable system that works consistently across packaging and digital touchpoints.
